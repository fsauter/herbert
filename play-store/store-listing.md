## English (`en-US` / `en-GB`)

### App name (26 / 30 chars)
```text
Remote Control for Herbert
```

### Short description (67 / 80 chars)
```text
Offline Bluetooth LE controller for Ponix Herbert vertical gardens.
```

### Full description
```text
Remote Control for Herbert is an independent, open-source Android companion app that keeps your Ponix Herbert vertical hydroponic garden working on modern Android devices — completely offline over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

KEY FEATURES
• Fast BLE discovery of nearby Herbert gardens (pull down to refresh)
• Live water-level status indicator (Empty, Low, OK, Full)
• LED brightness control (0–100%)
• Timer clock mode with customizable Sunrise & Sunset schedules, smooth fade transitions, and Vacation mode
• Manual light on/off toggle
• Automatic clock sync upon connecting
• Rename your garden (up to 15 characters) and attach a custom photo from camera or gallery
• Available in English and German

PRIVACY & OFFLINE BY DESIGN
• 100% offline — does not request INTERNET permission
• No accounts, no cloud servers, no analytics, and no ads
• All device names and custom photos stay strictly on your phone

IMPORTANT NOTES
• This app replaces the discontinued legacy app's local Bluetooth LE controls. Because the original manufacturer servers are offline, remote firmware updates and the online plant catalog are not included.
• Independent open-source project (MIT License), not affiliated with or endorsed by Ponix Systems: https://github.com/fsauter/herbert
```

```text
Initial release of Remote Control for Herbert:
• Full modern Android (12–17+) Bluetooth LE support
• Water level readout, brightness slider, timer schedules, smooth fade, vacation mode & manual switch
• 100% offline with zero data collection
```

```text
Fernbedienung für Herbert ist eine unabhängige Open-Source-App, mit der du deinen Ponix Herbert Hydroponik-Vertikalgarten auch auf modernen Android-Geräten zuverlässig nutzen kannst — komplett offline per Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).
```
